The Horse

They call him Flash.

01 · Ranch-Made

Built the old way—outside, horseback, every day.

Pepinstep is a powerful, keen-minded stallion with deep cow horse roots and proven ability in and out of the arena. Bred by the respected Flag Ranch and started by elite cow horse trainer Matt Koch, he carries both the physical tools and the mental edge to pass on to the next generation of great horses.

Koch picked him up as a yearling from the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity Sale and developed him from the ground up—branding, riding outside, and putting him to work with the kind of everyday cowboying that builds real-world, reliable horses.

02 · The Cow in Him

Calm mind. Athletic build. Natural cow sense.

Flash stood out early for the traits that can't be trained into a horse—a level head, an athletic frame, and instincts straight from the best cow horse blood in the book. Those traits pointed him straight toward the roping pen.

Enter World Champion Colby Lovell, who took him to the rope horse futurities to make him his next great rope horse stallion.

03 · The Champion

A commanding win at the Riata.

Flash made his mark at the 2023 Riata Championships, where Colby Lovell rode him to an emotional and commanding win in the #14.5 Championships—solidifying his place as one of the most exciting young rope horse stallions in the game.

Now standing for Theorem Ranch, Flash brings more than genetics to the table: a proven record, a willing heart, and the versatility that builds champions. With his size, structure, mind and grit, he's built to sire horses that go to work, stay level-headed, and win.

The 2027 Theorem Ranch Incentive

Pepinstep's foals are bred for the roping pen, and the Theorem Ranch Incentive pays there and beyond. A paid 2027 breeding to Flash qualifies one Theorem-sired horse in 2027 — an eligible champion at the Gold Buckle Futurities 4 & Under earns a $125,000 bonus, and the program runs all the way to a $500,000 bonus at the NCHA Open Futurity.

The short version

1

Buy a 2027 breeding to any Theorem Ranch stallion.

2

Register your Theorem-sired horse with Theorem Ranch at least 30 days before the event.

3

Finish in the top five overall at a qualifying futurity, and Theorem Ranch pays you a bonus on top of the event's prize money.

The bonus isn't automatic. Your horse has to finish in the top five against the whole field. If no eligible Theorem-sired horse finishes in the top five, no bonus is paid.

2027 futurity bonuses

These bonuses are paid on top of each event's own prize money. Placings are overall, against every horse in the finals, not just Theorem horses.

$100,000 guaranteed at the Riata Championships

This part of the program pays out no matter what — and it pays where Pepinstep made his name.

Theorem Ranch will pay $100,000 to the three Theorem-sired horses that earn the most at the 2027 Riata Championships:

  • $50,000 to the top-earning Theorem-sired horse
  • $30,000 to second
  • $20,000 to third

This is the only part of the program where your horse is measured against other Theorem-sired horses instead of the whole field.

To qualify, the horse needs a current Riata nomination, and the horse's owner or rider must have bought a 2027 Theorem Ranch breeding. The horse must also be registered with Theorem Ranch at least 30 days before the Riata Championships.

A share for the breeder

Every Theorem Ranch Incentive payment is split the same way:

  • One-third goes to the horse's breeder.
  • Two-thirds goes to the horse's owner, who can share it with the trainer or rider under their own private agreement.

Example: If a Theorem-sired horse wins the Gold Buckle 4 & Under Heeling, the $125,000 bonus is split about $83,333 to the owner and about $41,667 to the breeder. If you bred and own the horse, you keep all of it.

Payees are named when the horse is registered.

How to qualify, step by step

  1. Buy a 2027 breeding. It must be a paid 2027 breeding to Pepinstep, Stevie Rey Von, SEVS Judgement Day or Outta Yur League, for a mare you own. Fresh, frozen and ICSI breedings all count.
  2. Pick your horse. Any horse sired by a Theorem Ranch stallion can use your breeding. It doesn't have to be by the stallion you booked.
  3. Register the horse with Theorem Ranch at least 30 days before the event starts. Theorem Ranch must accept the registration.
  4. Compete. If your horse finishes in the top five overall in the finals, you earn the bonus.

One paid breeding qualifies one horse. Buy two breedings, and you can qualify two horses.

What doesn't count: Rebreeds, breedings bought from someone other than Theorem Ranch and complimentary breedings do not qualify unless Theorem Ranch agrees in writing.

Frequently asked questions

I'm breeding to Pepinstep in 2027. What does the incentive get me?

Your paid breeding qualifies one Theorem-sired horse you own at one 2027 event — a horse by Pepinstep, Stevie Rey Von, SEVS Judgement Day or Outta Yur League. Register the horse with Theorem Ranch at least 30 days before the event, and if it finishes in the top five overall, you earn the bonus.

Does a horse qualify just because it's by Pepinstep?

No. Being by a Theorem Ranch stallion makes a horse eligible to be registered. You still have to buy a 2027 breeding, register the horse on time, and it has to finish in the top five overall.

The basics

What is the Theorem Ranch Incentive?

It's a 2027 bonus program from Theorem Ranch. If you buy a 2027 breeding to a Theorem Ranch stallion and your Theorem-sired horse finishes in the top five at a qualifying futurity, Theorem Ranch pays a bonus on top of the event's prize money. It also guarantees $100,000 to the top three Theorem-sired horses at the 2027 Riata Championships.

Which events count?
  • NCHA Open Futurity
  • NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity (Open)
  • NRHA Futurity (Level 4 Open)
  • Gold Buckle Futurities World Finals: 4 & Under Heading, 4 & Under Heeling and 4 & Under Breakaway
  • Riata Championships (guaranteed $100,000 to the top three Theorem-sired horses)
Which stallions are in the program?

Pepinstep, Stevie Rey Von, SEVS Judgement Day and Outta Yur League.

Does this money come out of the event's purse?

No. Theorem Ranch pays it. It's added on top of whatever the event pays.

What years does it cover?

The program covers 2027 events.

Is it guaranteed?

Is the futurity money guaranteed?

No. The futurity bonuses are paid only if an eligible Theorem-sired horse finishes in the top five overall in the finals. If none does, no bonus is paid in that class.

Is the Riata money guaranteed?

Yes. Theorem Ranch will pay the full $100,000 at the 2027 Riata Championships, split $50,000, $30,000 and $20,000 among the three highest-earning eligible Theorem-sired horses.

Is it the top five Theorem horses, or the top five overall?

Top five overall. Your horse is placed against every horse in the finals, not just Theorem horses.

My horse was the highest-placing Theorem horse but finished seventh. Do I get a bonus?

No. A placing below fifth pays nothing.

Does owning or riding a Theorem-sired horse earn a bonus by itself?

No. The owner has to buy a 2027 breeding, register the horse on time, and the horse has to finish in the top five.

Qualifying

What do I have to buy?

A paid 2027 breeding to a Theorem Ranch stallion, for a mare you own.

Does the horse I compete on have to be by the stallion I booked?

No. It has to be sired by one of the four Theorem Ranch stallions, but it can be any of them. Example: You book a mare to Pepinstep and use that breeding to qualify a Stevie Rey Von 3-year-old at the NCHA Futurity.

Do frozen and ICSI breedings count?

Yes. Fresh, frozen and ICSI breedings all count.

Do rebreeds count?

No, unless Theorem Ranch agrees in writing.

I bought a breeding from another breeder, not from Theorem Ranch. Does it count?

No, unless Theorem Ranch agrees in writing.

What about a free or complimentary breeding?

It doesn't count unless Theorem Ranch agrees in writing.

Can one breeding qualify more than one horse?

No. One paid breeding qualifies one horse. Buy two breedings to qualify two horses.

Can one breeding cover my horse at more than one event?

No. One breeding covers one event. To qualify your horse at a second event, you need a second breeding.

When do I have to register?

At least 30 days before the event starts. If you miss the deadline, your horse is not eligible, even if it wins.

How do I register?

Fill out the registration form at COMING SOON and send it to Theorem Ranch. Your horse is eligible once Theorem Ranch accepts the registration.

Do I still have to enter the event the normal way?

Yes. This program doesn't replace any event's entry, nomination or eligibility rules. Your horse still has to be eligible for and entered in the event on its own.

Does my horse have to be enrolled in Gold Buckle's Elite Stallion program or any other incentive?

No. The Theorem Ranch Incentive is separate from every event's own stallion or breeder incentives.

Getting paid

Who gets the money?

One-third goes to the horse's breeder. Two-thirds goes to the owner, who splits it with the trainer or rider under their own agreement.

Who counts as the breeder?

The breeder of record on the horse's AQHA papers.

I bred and own the horse. Do I get the breeder's share too?

Yes. If you're both the breeder and the owner, you receive the full bonus.

Does Theorem Ranch pay my rider or trainer directly?

No. The owner receives the owner's two-thirds and handles any split with the rider or trainer.

What happens if horses tie?

If an eligible horse ties for a top-five place, the bonus amounts for the tied placing and the next lower placing(s) are added together and split evenly among the tied horses. Only eligible Theorem-sired horses receive a share.

What if my horse is disqualified?

A disqualified horse earns no bonus. If a bonus was already paid, it must be paid back.

Team roping and breakaway

Do heading and heeling split the $125,000?

No. They're separate. The champion head horse can earn $125,000, and the champion heel horse can earn another $125,000.

My partner and I both ride Theorem-sired horses. Can we both get paid?

Yes. The 4 & Under Heading and 4 & Under Heeling are separate classes with separate bonuses, so a head horse and a heel horse can each earn one. Each horse has to be eligible and registered on its own, and each horse's owner needs their own 2027 breeding.

Which Gold Buckle event counts?

The 2027 Gold Buckle Futurities World Finals: the 4 & Under Heading, the 4 & Under Heeling and the 4 & Under Breakaway.

Does the Riata money include breakaway?

No. The Riata Championships are team roping only.

I rope at the Riata on a Theorem-sired horse, but I don't own it. Can I qualify?

Yes, for the Riata money. If you bought a 2027 Theorem Ranch breeding and the horse has a current Riata nomination, it can qualify. For the Gold Buckle bonuses, the horse's owner must hold the breeding.

Is the Riata money paid by division?

No. It goes to the three Theorem-sired horses with the highest earnings at the 2027 Riata Championships. Earnings from every class count, including the Rope Horse Rewards ropings.

What if fewer than three Theorem-sired horses qualify at the Riata?

All of the money is paid out even if fewer than three qualified Theorem-sired horses compete — Theorem Ranch pays ground money.
